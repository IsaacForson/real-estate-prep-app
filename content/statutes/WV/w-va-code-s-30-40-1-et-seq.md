COMMISSION FOREWORD 
 
The West Virginia Real Estate License Act (WV Code §30-40-1 et 
seq.) has the principal purpose of protecting the public against 
unscrupulous practices of real estate salespersons. The law mandates 
that the Real Estate Commission shall do all things necessary and 
convenient for carrying into effect the provisions of WV Code §30-
40-1 et seq., and may from time to time promulgate reasonable, fair 
and impartial rules and regulations. To comply with this mandate 
the Commission has promulgated legislative rules Title 174 Series 
1, Series 2, Series 3, Series 4, Series 6, Series 7 and Interpretative 
Series 5.
