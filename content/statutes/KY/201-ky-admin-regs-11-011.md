class="section" data-headers="0:1:2" data-level="1"><h2 class="headers" aria-level="3">(3)</h2><span class="section-text">"Affiliated licensee" means a sales associate or a broker who is affiliated data-level="1"><h2 class="headers" aria-level="3">(5)</h2><span class="section-text">"Agency consent agreement" means the form prescribed by the commission and used by a principal broker and a client to establish the agency relationship in writing.</span></section><section class="section" data-headers="0:1:5" data-level="1"><h2 class="headers" aria-level="3">(6)</h2><span
