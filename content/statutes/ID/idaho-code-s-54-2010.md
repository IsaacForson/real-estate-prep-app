Idaho Code Title 54, Chapter 20 — Idaho Real Estate License Law

TITLE 54
PROFESSIONS, VOCATIONS, AND BUSINESSES
CHAPTER 20
IDAHO REAL ESTATE LICENSE LAW
54-2010.
Compensation.
Members of the education council are not employees of the state of Idaho, but shall be reimbursed expenses in the same manner as state employees in addition to a per diem allowance in the same amount as that received by the commissioners for each day of approved service.
