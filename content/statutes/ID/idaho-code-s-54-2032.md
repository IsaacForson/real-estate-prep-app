Idaho Code Title 54, Chapter 20 — Idaho Real Estate License Law

TITLE 54
PROFESSIONS, VOCATIONS, AND BUSINESSES
CHAPTER 20
IDAHO REAL ESTATE LICENSE LAW
54-2032.
Certification of instructors.
All individuals wishing to teach real estate courses for credit toward prelicense, post license or the commission continuing education core course requirements in Idaho must first be approved or certified by the commission for each course the individual wishes to teach.
